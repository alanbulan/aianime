# v1.1.3

## User-facing Highlights (zh)

- **Electron 桌面开发模式**: Electron 直接启动 Vite 与本地 FastAPI，前端修改可通过 HMR 即时生效。
- **全局深浅主题**: 项目、素材、分镜、画布、弹窗和任务中心统一支持清晰的浅色与深色显示。
- **版本与发布通知**: 恢复版本说明、更新提醒、发布通知抽屉和 Chunk 加载失败恢复。

## User-facing Highlights (en)

- **Electron desktop development**: Electron starts Vite and the local FastAPI service directly, with HMR for frontend changes.
- **Complete light and dark themes**: Projects, assets, storyboards, canvas tools, dialogs, and tasks now share consistent theme colors.
- **Version and release notifications**: Restores release notes, update prompts, the notification drawer, and chunk-load recovery.
